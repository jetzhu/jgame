HORDE GATE

How to play

  • Your squad runs and fires on its own. Drag left or right (or use ← → / A D) to steer. Hold ↑ / W to run at double speed: you reach gates sooner, but you also get less time to shoot.
  • Gates change your squad: + and × are good, − and ÷ are bad. Take one gate from each row.
  • Every soldier fires, so a bigger squad hits harder. Each enemy that reaches you takes soldiers with it. Elites (big, horned, with a health bar) lead each horde and take six soldiers if they reach you.
  • Vaults (concrete blocks with a number) guard a lane of gold gates. Watch them crack, shoot them open first, then run through. Once inside, the walls keep you in.
  • Slabs: a field of gates raised on a concrete slab, with the horde hiding in the garage underneath. You can't get past while it's up: hold the line, survive the horde, and shoot the pillar. The slab slams down, crushing everything still under it, and the whole field is yours. The gold column is on the pillar's side.
  • The tide: every third level you start with a single soldier in front of a packed horde that fills the road and creeps at you. You can't walk past it. Chew your way up the ladder of small +1 plates along the rail, or shoot open the sacks plugging the walled gold lane on the other side and run past it all. While you shoot the sacks, the tide keeps coming.
  • Pump gates (solid boards with a striped frame and a bullseye on top) get better while you shoot them; plain see-through gates never change. Crates give power-ups when shot open. Barrels explode: shoot them in a crowd, don't run into them.
  • Every fifth level ends with a boss. When a red circle appears on the ground, steer out of it before the fists come down. Survivors at the finish climb the bonus ramp for up to ×5 coins.
  • Spend coins on upgrades. Losing still pays a little, so you can always get stronger and try again.
  • Endless keeps going until you are overrun. How far can you get?

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your progress (level, coins, upgrades, best distance) is saved only in your browser's local storage.
